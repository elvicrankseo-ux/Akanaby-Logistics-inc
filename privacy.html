Privacy policy

Last updated: August 2026

Akanaby Logistics Inc. ("we", "us") operates this website. This page explains what information we collect when you contact us or use the site, and what we do with it.

Information we collect

  • Information you give us. When you submit a quote request or contact form, or call or email us, you may provide your name, phone number, email address, vehicle details and the location of your vehicle.
  • Information collected automatically. Like most websites, our hosting and analytics tools may record technical information such as IP address, browser type, device type, referring page and the pages you view.

How we use it

  • To respond to your request, quote a job and dispatch a truck.
  • To contact you about a service you asked for.
  • To keep records required for our business and insurance.
  • To understand how the site is used so we can improve it.

What we do not do

We do not sell your personal information. We do not share it with third parties for their own marketing.

Who we may share information with

We may share limited information with service providers who help us operate the business — for example, our website host, form delivery provider, phone provider or analytics provider — and with your insurer, repair shop or law enforcement where that is part of the job you asked us to do or where the law requires it.

Cookies and analytics

This site may use cookies and similar technologies for basic functionality and for analytics. You can block or delete cookies in your browser settings; some parts of the site may not work as intended if you do.

Third-party content

Some pages load Google Maps. When you choose to load a map, Google may receive information about your visit under its own privacy policy.

Data retention

We keep job and contact records for as long as we need them for the service, our accounting, and any legal or insurance requirement, and then delete them.

Your choices

You can ask us what information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing info.akanaby@gmail.com or calling (351) 235-8976.

Children

This site is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If we change this policy we will update the date at the top of this page.
