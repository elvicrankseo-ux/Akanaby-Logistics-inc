Flatbed towing

All four wheels off the ground — the safest way to move low, luxury, AWD and damaged vehicles.

Flatbed towing in Lowell protects transmissions, bumpers and lowered suspensions. It is our default for all-wheel drive, EVs, classics and anything that has been in a collision. Call now →

Roadside assistance

Flat tyre, dead battery, no fuel — often we can get you moving without a tow at all.

Roadside assistance covers tyre changes with your spare, jump starts, fuel delivery and lockouts. If the fix works, you drive away. If it doesn’t, the same truck can tow you. Call now →

Jump starts

A flat battery in a cold Lowell winter is our most common call. We carry professional boosters.

We use proper jump packs rather than cables off another car, so there is no risk to your electronics. If the battery will not hold a charge, we’ll tow you to a garage instead. Call now →

Fuel delivery

Ran the tank dry? We bring enough fuel to get you to the nearest station.

Tell dispatch whether you need gas or diesel and roughly where you are — a mile marker or cross street is enough. We deliver, you drive off. Call now →

Vehicle lockouts

Keys locked inside? We open the door without damaging your paint, glass or trim.

We use professional lockout tools and ask for proof the vehicle is yours before opening it — that protects everybody. Please call right away if a child or pet is inside. Call now →

Winch-out recovery

Stuck in snow, mud, sand or a ditch? Our winch gear pulls you back onto solid ground.

New England winters put plenty of cars off the shoulder. We assess the angle and surface first, then winch out with the right rigging so the recovery does not add new damage. Call now →

Accident recovery

After a collision we clear the vehicle safely and calmly, and work with police on scene.

Make sure everyone is safe and emergency services are called first, then call us. We load damaged vehicles on a flatbed and take them wherever you or your insurer direct. Call now →

Vehicle transport

Auction buys, project cars, seasonal vehicles — scheduled transport on a flatbed.

Book transport in advance and we’ll agree a pickup window that suits you. Non-running vehicles are fine — just let us know so we bring the winch. Call now →

Fleet services

If downtime costs you money, set up an account with us. Your drivers call one number, we know your vehicles, and billing is handled at the office rather than roadside. Call now →

Dealership towing

Private property towing
